How to Add a Product

A simple step-by-step guide for shop staff — ZIC POS

Adding a product takes about a minute. You enter the item once, set up the pack sizes you sell it in (single, box, carton…), and the system handles all the counting and price conversions for you.

1Open the form

Go to Admin → Products and click the + Add Product button at the top right. A window pops up.

2Fill the basic info

Field What to type Example
Product Name The name of the item Chocolate Cookies
Barcode Scan with the camera button, or click Gen to create one ZIC123456789
Category Pick from the list (or leave as “None”) Snacks
Tip: No barcode on the item? Just click Gen and the system makes a unique one for you.

3Set your default prices

  • Default Purchase Price — what you pay for one single piece (your cost).
  • Default Sale Price — what the customer pays for one single piece.

These are the prices for your base unit (one piece). You’ll set bigger-pack prices in the next step.

4Set up your units (the important part)

The Units, Conversion & Pricing table lets you sell the same product in different pack sizes.

The base unit is your smallest sellable piece — all stock is counted in it. By default it’s called Piece, but you can rename it (e.g. “Bottle”, “Egg”, “Kg”).

To add a bigger pack (like a Box):

  1. Click + Add Unit.
  2. Unit Name → type the pack name, e.g. Box.
  3. Equals (in Pieces) → how many pieces are inside. A box of 6 → type 6.
  4. Purchase & Sale → the cost and selling price for the whole box.
  5. The Profit column fills in automatically.

Example setup:

Unit Equals Purchase Sale Profit
Piece (Base) 1 8 10 2
Box 6 45 60 15
Carton 24 170 230 60
The small badges under each name let you Set base (mark the smallest piece — only one can be base) and pick the Default sale unit (what the cashier sees first at checkout).

5Enter your starting stock

In the Initial Stock box, pick any unit and type how much you have — the system converts it for you.

  • Received 2 Cartons? Pick Carton, type 2.
  • It instantly shows Total Stock = 48 Pieces (2 × 24).

You never have to count single pieces — enter stock whichever way is easiest.

6Check the preview (optional)

  • Stock Summary — shows your stock in neat chunks (e.g. 2 Cartons).
  • Real-time Stock Preview — test “If you sell 1 Box” and see what’s left. It warns you in red if stock would go negative.

7Save

Click the green ✓ Save Product button at the bottom. Done! Your product is ready to sell.

Quick rules to remember

  • Stock is always stored in the base unit (pieces).
  • You can sell in any unit — the system converts and subtracts stock automatically.
  • Each unit can have its own price.
  • Unit names must be unique, and conversion must be greater than 0.
  • Purchase and sale prices cannot be negative.
